# BiztechCS Brand Guide — Social Media Video & Image
### Version 2.0 · October 2026 · matches biztechcs.com

For anyone making posts, carousels, thumbnails, reels and short videos. Not a development spec.

---

## 1. The brand in one line

**Biztech** (BiztechCS) — *An AI-forward software engineering and products company. ERP, custom software, cloud and AI, delivered as outcomes.*

- Write **Biztech** or **BiztechCS**. Never "Biztech CS" or "BizTech".
- The old line "Tech that works, so you don't have to." is retired. Don't use it.

## 2. Logo

Files in [`assets/`](assets/): `biztech-logo.svg` (mark + Biztech®) · `biztech-mark.svg` (mark only).

| Background | Use |
|---|---|
| White / light grey | Full colour (teal mark, dark grey wordmark) |
| Ink, dark gradient, photo | All-white version (export it white; don't tint) |
| Teal fill | All-white version |

- Clear space: the height of the **B** on all sides.
- Minimum: **110 px** wide on a 1080 canvas; **210 px** on a 1920 video frame. Mark only below that.
- Place top-left or bottom-left. One logo per frame.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, recolour, or put the teal mark on teal.

| ⬜ | **Mist** | `#F5F7FB` | Soft light background |
| ▫️ | **Line** | `#E4E8F0` | Thin borders |

Text on light: `#232B3A` body, `#5E6779` secondary. Text on ink: white headlines, `#AEB8CA` body.

**Dark background:** gradient `#10262B → #204049` at 150°, with a faint teal glow (`#35B0BF` at ~15%) top-right.
**Light background:** mist `#F5F7FB` with the same faint glow.

Mix: mostly paper/mist or ink, teal as the accent (buttons, one highlighted word, icons). No yellow, orange, navy blue or purple.

## 5. Layout

- **Formats:** 1080 × 1080 (feed), 1080 × 1350 (carousel), 1080 × 1920 (story / reel), 1920 × 1080 (YouTube / LinkedIn video), 1280 × 720 thumbnail.
- **Margins:** 80 px on a 1080 canvas, 120 px on 1920. Keep text inside.
- **Corners:** 40 px radius on cards and image frames (1080 canvas); 24 px on buttons; pills fully round.
- **Shadows:** soft and low, teal-tinted (`#0E7C88` at 30%, large blur, offset down). Never hard black.
- **One frame, one idea:** eyebrow → headline (highlight one phrase in teal) → sub-line or one stat → CTA. Left-aligned.
- **Cards:** white, 1 px `#E4E8F0` border, 40 px radius, icon in a 90 px teal-tint chip (`#E6F5F7`).
- **Stats:** big white number on ink, unit in bright teal, label in `#AEB8CA` below.

## 6. Imagery & icons

- Real product screens, real client logos, candid team and event photos. No stock handshakes, no "hands on laptop", no 3D blobs.
- Client / partner logos in grey on white tiles (full colour only when it's the point of the post).
- Icons: outline style, 1.7 px stroke on a 24 grid (Lucide / Feather look), teal `#0E7C88` inside a light-teal chip. One icon per card. No emoji as icons.
- Photos on dark frames get an ink wash (`#10262B` at 45%) so text stays readable.

## 7. Video

- Inter only. Ink or mist backgrounds, teal accents. Same type sizes as the 1920 column above.
- Logo: white, static, top-left (80, 54), 210 px wide. Hidden only on the end card.
- Motion: quick and soft. Text rises 24 px and fades in over 0.5–0.7 s; cards lift 3–6 px; buttons never bounce. Easing ease-out. No spins, flashes or film grain.
- Every spoken number lands on screen as a stat at the moment it's said.
- Captions on every talking frame; the video must work on mute.
- **End card (3 s):** ink gradient, white logo centred, headline "Ready to build something that actually works?", teal button **Book a 30-Min Call →**, `biztechcs.com` in `#AEB8CA`.

## 8. Words

**Voice:** short, plain, confident. Two beats, full stops. Name the risk, then the fix.
"Most AI stalls at the pilot. We take it the last mile." · "One Partner. Every Layer." · "Common questions. Straight answers."

**Stats to use (exact):** 19+ Years · 1,300+ Projects · 98% Client Retention · 4.7/5 Clutch · 200+ Team · 800+ Clients · ISO 27001 & 9001 · Odoo Ready Partner

**Eyebrows:** WHAT WE DO · HOW WE DELIVER · AI & DATA · OUR WORK · WHY BIZTECH · CASE STUDY · EVENT · NEXT STEP

**CTAs:** Book a 30-Min Call → · Let's Connect · See our work · Explore AI & Data → · Read more →
Avoid: Submit, Click here, Learn more, Talk to Experts, Get Started.

**Hashtags / handle:** @biztechcs · #BiztechCS
