Tone & Voice

Confident, short, concrete. Two-beat sentences with full stops. Name the risk before the promise: "Most AI stalls at the pilot. We take it the last mile."

  • Lead with the outcome, then say how
  • Numbers over adjectives: 1,300+ projects, not "countless"
  • Admit the hard part: pilots stall, partners get switched, legacy is messy
  • One next step on every page: "Book a 30-Min Call"

Visual Style Summary

A light, editorial enterprise aesthetic: white and mist surfaces, deep ink for headings and dark bands, and a single teal family for everything interactive. One typeface at four weights, large radii (18–20px cards, 11px buttons), hairline borders, soft teal-tinted shadows, and proof everywhere: stat bars, badges, grayscale client logos that colourise on hover, real client video testimonials.

Section 02

Logo Guidelines

The Mark — Live Files

A teal swoosh mark (three interlocking crescents, #35B0BF) followed by the Biztech® wordmark in dark grey (#373435, #5E5E5E detail). The ® is part of the lockup. Files: assets/biztech-logo.svg (406 × 95, from /images/brand/logo.svg) and assets/biztech-mark.svg (favicon).

Full colour — paper (header, 128 × 30 px)
Reversed — ink (footer, 203 × 47 px, via filter: brightness(0) invert(1))
Full colour — mist sections
Reversed — teal fills (never the teal mark on teal)
Mark only — favicon, app icon, avatars
Mark keeps its teal on ink
There is no separate white logo file on the site; the reverse is a CSS filter on the same SVG. Export a true white version for print and video.

Clear Space & Minimum Size

Keep a clear zone equal to the height of the capital B on all four sides. The 72 px header leaves ≥ 21 px above and below the 30 px logo.

ApplicationMinimum width
Web header (as shipped)128px
Any digital placement110px
Print25mm
Favicon / app icon32px (mark only)
Video corner logo210px on a 1920 canvas

Incorrect Usage

  • Do not recolour the mark outside teal #35B0BF or white
  • Do not set the wordmark in Inter or any font; always use the SVG
  • Do not separate the ® from the wordmark
  • Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows / glows
  • Do not place the full-colour logo on ink or teal surfaces
  • Do not pair the logo with the retired "Tech that works…" tagline

Section 12

Imagery & Illustration Style

Photography

  • Testimonials: real client video thumbnails, person facing camera, natural light, 16:9, play overlay
  • Case studies: product screenshots and storefronts over people shots; show the real UI
  • Events: candid booth / conference photos, 4:3 inside the event card
  • People: team photos only on About / Careers, natural colour
  • No staged stock handshakes, no generic "hands on laptop" imagery

Logos, badges & abstract visuals

  • Client logos monochrome at rest (grayscale(1), 65%), colour on hover, in 150 × 92 cards
  • Certification badges full colour on white 72px tiles inside the stat bar
  • Teal radial glows at ≤ 13% alpha on mist; no colour blobs, no 3D renders
  • Diagrams use the card language: white tiles, --line borders, teal connectors, ink labels
  • UI mockups flat and front-on with the 18px card radius and --shadow

Focus & keyboard

  • :focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--indigo); outline-offset: 3px; border-radius: 4px }; switch to --teal on dark bands

  • Mega menus open on :focus-within
  • Drawer: aria-expanded on the burger, Escape closes, focus returns
  • Accordions: <button aria-expanded aria-controls>; one H1, sections H2, cards H4

  • Running text ≥ 16px; 13px only for meta, labels, legal

  • Uppercase only on 13–14px eyebrows and pills
  • Visible labels; errors as text + border; status via aria-live="polite"
  • Real alt on client logos ("TVS Motor"); decorative SVGs aria-hidden

Headlines

  • Write in sentence case; CSS renders Title Case on H1–H4

Section 19

Do's and Don'ts

Visual & colour

Do
  • Alternate paper → mist → dark gradient sections
  • 18px card radius, 11px button radius, everywhere
  • Long soft teal-tinted shadows
  • --indigo for every button, link and interactive stroke
  • --ink for headings and dark bands
  • Reserve amber for large stat numbers
Don't
  • Stack two dark bands or two mist sections
  • Mix 6px / 8px / 12px radii from the old system
  • Hard grey drop shadows
  • --teal as text on white, or white text on teal
  • Pure black, the old navy #0B1F3A, or old greys like #6B7280
  • Amber, orange or yellow buttons

Typography & content

Do
  • Inter only, weights 400–700
  • clamp() scale for H1 / H2, tight tracking
  • Uppercase only on 13–14px eyebrows and pills
  • H2 ≤ 40ch, lead ≤ 82ch
  • Short two-beat headlines with full stops; real names and numbers
  • Footer descriptor as the brand line
Don't
  • Reintroduce Poppins, or use weight 800 on the web
  • Hard-code 56px headings
  • Uppercase body copy or buttons
  • Headlines with exclamation marks
  • "A leading retailer", "significant gains"
  • "Tech that works, so you don't have to."

Buttons & CTAs

Do
  • One primary per section, ghost beside it
  • "Book a 30-Min Call" as the page-level CTA
  • → after labels that go somewhere
  • Full-width primary inside forms
Don't
  • Two primaries side by side
  • "Talk to Experts", "Get Started", "Submit"
  • Arrows on "Let's Connect"
  • Ghost or secondary submit buttons

How the Visual System Works

Ink #10262B

The authority: headings, stat bar, dark bands, footer.

Action teal #0E7C88

The only interactive colour: buttons, links, kickers, icons, focus.

Bright teal #35B0BF

The signature: logo mark, stat units, eyebrows on dark, hover accents.
