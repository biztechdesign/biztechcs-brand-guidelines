## 1. Brand Overview

### Brand Name
**Biztech Consulting & Solutions** (abbreviated as **BiztechCS**). Legal entity: **Biztech® IT Consultancy Pvt. Ltd.** The wordmark reads simply **Biztech®**.

### Brand Positioning
BiztechCS is **"an AI-forward software engineering and products company."** The site states it as outcomes, not capabilities: *ERP, custom software, cloud and AI, delivered as outcomes.*

The home page hero makes the positioning concrete:

> **AI-forward engineering & products · since 2006**
> **Engineering [measurable outcomes · AI agents · modern ERP · data platforms · digital growth] for the AI-first enterprise.**

The bracketed phrase rotates. Everything else on the page serves one idea: AI that reaches production, owned end to end by one partner.

> **Tagline status.** "Tech that works, so you don't have to." (v1.0) does not appear anywhere on the redesigned site. The line in use is the footer descriptor: *An AI-forward software engineering and products company.* Do not reintroduce the old tagline on new web, social, or video assets without sign-off.

Certifications & alliances shown on the site: **ISO 27001 & ISO 9001 (TÜV SÜD certified)** · **Great Place to Work Certified** · **Odoo Ready Partner** · **Clutch verified reviews**.

### Tone and Voice

Confident, short, concrete. The site writes in two-beat sentences and ends them with full stops: *"Hear it from them. In their own words."* It names the risk before the promise: *"Most AI stalls at the pilot. We take it the last mile."*

- **Lead with the outcome**, then say how
- **Numbers over adjectives:** 1,300+ projects, not "countless"
- **Admit the hard part:** pilots stall, partners get switched, legacy is messy
- **One clear next step** on every page: "Book a 30-Min Call"

Dense with proof: stat bars, certification badges, grayscale client logos that colorize on hover, and real client testimonials with video.

## 2. Logo Guidelines

### The Mark

The logo is a **teal swoosh mark** (three interlocking crescents, `#35B0BF`) followed by the **"Biztech®"** wordmark in a dark grey (`#373435`, with `#5E5E5E` detail). The registered symbol is part of the lockup.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| [`assets/biztech-logo.svg`](../assets/biztech-logo.svg) | Full horizontal lockup, 406 × 95 viewBox (from `/images/brand/logo.svg`) |
| [`assets/biztech-mark.svg`](../assets/biztech-mark.svg) | Mark only, square, used as favicon (from `/favicon.svg`) |

### Variations in use on the site

1. **Full colour horizontal** — header, 128 × 30 px at desktop (teal mark + dark wordmark on white)
2. **Reversed (all-white)** — footer, 203 × 47 px, produced with `filter: brightness(0) invert(1)` on the same SVG
3. **Mark only** — favicon, apple-touch-icon, social avatars

There is no separate white logo file on the site; the reverse is a CSS filter. For print or video, export a white version from the SVG rather than relying on the filter.

### Clear Space

Keep a clear zone equal to the **height of the capital "B"** on all four sides. The header leaves ≥ 21 px above and below the 30 px logo inside a 72 px bar.

```
        X
    ┌──────────┐
 X  │ (mark) Biztech® │  X
    └──────────┘
        X
```

### Minimum Size

| Application | Minimum width |
|---|---|
| Web header | 128 px (as shipped) |
| Any digital placement | 110 px |
| Print | 25 mm |
| Favicon / app icon | 32 px (mark only) |
| Video corner logo | 210 px on a 1920 canvas |

### Backgrounds

| Background | Version |
|---|---|
| White / Mist `#F5F7FB` | Full colour |
| Ink `#10262B` / dark gradient bands | Reversed white |
| Teal `#0E7C88` or `#35B0BF` fills | Reversed white (never the teal mark on teal) |
| Photography | Reversed white over a dark scrim |

### Incorrect Usage

- Do not recolour the mark outside teal `#35B0BF` or white
- Do not set the wordmark in Inter or any other font; always use the SVG
- Do not separate the ® from the wordmark
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows / glows
- Do not place the full-colour logo on ink or teal surfaces
- Do not pair the logo with the retired "Tech that works…" tagline

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Font Family

**Inter, and only Inter.** Both `--font-display` and `--font-body` resolve to `"Inter", system-ui, sans-serif`. The site self-hosts Inter as `woff2` at weights **400, 500, 600, 700** with `font-display: swap`. Weight 800 is not loaded; do not use it on the web.

```css
--font-body:    "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-display: "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-mono:    ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace;   /* code only */
```

### 4.2 Heading Scale

| Token | Element | Size | Weight | Line height | Letter spacing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--fs-h1` | **H1** | `clamp(34px, 4.5vw, 56px)` | `700` | `1.08` | `-0.025em` | Hero only; rotating phrase in `--indigo` |
| `--fs-h2` | **H2** | `clamp(28px, 3.6vw, 40px)` | `700` | `1.2` | `-0.02em` | Section titles, `max-width: 40ch` |

| `--fs-h4` | **H4** | `20px` | `600` | `1.5` | `0` | Card titles, blog titles, mega-menu feature |

## 5. Layout System

### 5.1 Container (`.wrap`)

One container class, centred, `padding: 0 20px`, with a stepped max-width:

| Viewport | `.wrap` max-width |
|---|---|
| < 1200px | `1130px` |
| ≥ 1200px | `1152px` |
| ≥ 1440px | `1250px` → `1330px` |
| ≥ 1600px | `1472px` |

The same widths apply to `.mega-inner` (mega menu) and `.sn-panel-inner` (sticky nav panels), so dropdown content aligns with page content.

### 5.2 Breakpoints

```css
/* used in the live stylesheet, most to least frequent */
@media (max-width: 1080px)   /* tablet & below: stacks most grids, shows burger */
@media (max-width: 640px)    /* phone: single column, 16px lead, 40px sections */
@media (max-width: 1200px)
@media (max-width: 768px)
@media (max-width: 480px)
@media (min-width: 1440px)
@media (min-width: 1600px)
```

**1080px is the desktop/mobile switch** for navigation and most grids. Use it, not 1024px.

### 5.3 Grid Patterns

| Context | Desktop | ≤ 1080px | ≤ 640px | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | `7fr 5fr` (copy / visual) | 1 col | 1 col | 40px |
| Feature cards (AI, engagement) | `repeat(4, 1fr)` | 2 | 1 | 14–20px |
| Industry cards | `repeat(3–4, 1fr)` | 2 | 1 | 14px |
| Testimonials / events / blog | `repeat(3, 1fr)` | 1 col, `max-width: 620px` centred | 1 | 20px |
| Stat bar | `repeat(5, 1fr)` | 2 col | 2 col | 20px |
| FAQ | `1fr 1fr`, `max-width: 1000px` | 1 | 1 | `0 40px` |
| Footer offices | `repeat(4, 1fr)` | 2 | 1 | 30px |
| Mega menu | `280px repeat(3, 1fr)` (feature card + 3 columns) | drawer | drawer | 32px |

### 5.4 Section Spacing (`--sec-pad`)

| Viewport | Vertical padding |
|---|---|
| < 768px | `40px` |
| ≥ 768px | `50px` |
| ≥ 1600px | `70px` |

Section head (`.sec-head`) margin-bottom: `56px` desktop, `30px` mobile. Section titles have `margin-top: 18px` from the kicker; sub-copy `16px` below the title.

### 5.5 Section Backgrounds (alternate, in this order)

1. **Paper** `#FFFFFF`
2. **Mist** `#F5F7FB` (`.bg-mist`)
3. **Hero mist** — `#F5F7FB` plus two radial teal glows:
   `radial-gradient(1200px 520px at 80% -14%, rgba(53,176,191,.13), transparent 60%), radial-gradient(1000px 480px at 4% 2%, rgba(14,124,136,.10), transparent 60%)`, with a `1px --line` bottom border
4. **Dark band** — `linear-gradient(150deg, #10262B, #204049)` (Odoo band, feature panels)
5. **Stat bar** — solid `#10262B`
6. **CTA band** — `linear-gradient(150deg, #10262B, #173139)` + top-centre teal glow

Never put two dark bands back to back.

### 5.6 Alignment

- **Left-aligned** by default: hero, section heads, cards, footer
- **Centred** (`.sec-head.center`): proof sections only — testimonials, industries, FAQ, logos
- Full-bleed: hero, stat bar, dark bands, footer

## 7. Button Guidelines

### 7.1 Base (`.btn`)

```css
.btn {
  display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 9px;
  font-family: var(--font-body); font-weight: 600; font-size: 15.5px; line-height: 1.5;
  padding: 14px 24px; border-radius: 11px;
  transition: .15s;
}
.btn .arr { transition: transform .2s; }
.btn:hover .arr { transform: translateX(4px); }
.btn:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--indigo); outline-offset: 3px; border-radius: 11px; }
```

Arrows are a literal `→` inside `<span class="arr">`, placed after the label.

### 7.2 Primary (`.btn-primary`)

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | `--indigo` `#0E7C88` |
| Text | `#FFFFFF` |
| Shadow | `0 14px 30px -14px rgba(14,124,136,.6)` |
| **Hover** | background `--indigo-600` `#0A6772`, `translateY(-1px)` |
| Height | 51 px |

**Usage:** "Book a 30-Min Call →", "Explore AI & Data →", "Explore Odoo services →", "Send message →". One primary per section. Also the only button style used inside forms (full width).

```css
.btn-primary { background: var(--indigo); color: #fff; box-shadow: 0 14px 30px -14px rgba(14,124,136,.6); }
.btn-primary:hover { background: var(--indigo-600); transform: translateY(-1px); }
```

### 7.3 Ghost (`.btn-ghost`)

The secondary button. On light surfaces:

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | `#FFFFFF` |
| Text | `--ink` |
| Border | `1px solid --line` |
| Padding | `13px 24px` |
| **Hover** | border and text `--indigo` |

| `--indigo #0E7C88` on `--mist` | 4.6:1 | AA (just; prefer white behind 13 px teal text) |
| `--indigo-600 #0A6772` on `--teal-tint` | 5.9:1 | AA (pills) |

**Rules:** bright teal `#35B0BF` is never used for text on white or mist, and never as a fill behind white text. Use `--indigo` for text and buttons, `--teal` for marks, units, and text on dark. Amber is for large stat numbers only.

### 15.2 Focus

```css
:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--indigo); outline-offset: 3px; border-radius: 4px; }

```

Never remove outlines without this replacement. On dark bands switch the outline to `--teal`.

### 15.3 Keyboard & Structure

- Skip link is the first focusable element
- Mega menus open on `:focus-within`, so keyboard users get the same panel
- Mobile drawer: `aria-expanded` on the burger, `Escape` closes, focus returns to the burger
- Accordions: `<button aria-expanded aria-controls>`; one H1 per page; sections use H2, cards use H4

### 15.4 Text

- Minimum running text 16 px; 13 px reserved for meta, labels and legal
- Line length capped: titles `40ch`, sub-copy `82ch`, hero lead ~681 px
- Uppercase only for 13–14 px eyebrows and pills, never for paragraphs

### 15.5 Forms & Media

- Every input has a visible `<label>`; required fields marked in the label text
- Errors in text (`.field-error`) plus border colour, announced via `aria-live="polite"` on the status line
- Video testimonials have titles in `alt`/`aria-label` ("Fernando Delgado, CIO…")
- Client logos have real `alt` text ("TVS Motor", "PwC"); decorative SVGs are `aria-hidden`

| **Direct** | "AI generates; senior engineers own every merge." |
| **Two-beat** | "One Partner. Every Layer." · "Common questions. Straight answers." |
| **Honest** | "Most AI stalls at the pilot. We take it the last mile." |
| **Proven** | "Why businesses choose us, and stay." (then the 98%) |
| **Human** | "Hear it from them. In their own words." |
| **Plain** | "Ready to build something that actually works?" |

### 16.2 Headline Rules

- Write in **sentence case**; the CSS renders Title Case on H1–H4
- 4–10 words. Full stops are welcome in headlines; exclamation marks are not
- Two short sentences beat one long one: "Your team. Your process. Your outcomes."
- Name the thing, not the category: "Odoo", "AI agents", "data platforms", never "solutions"
- H1 formula on landing pages: *[Verb] [specific outcome] for [specific audience].*

### 16.3 Body Copy

- Lead paragraph ≤ 3 sentences, 18 px
- Card bodies ≤ 2 sentences, 16 px
- Use "you" and "we"; never "our clients" when you mean "you"
- Numbers as numerals with `+`: 19+, 1,300+, 98%, 4.7/5. Keep the comma in 1,300
- American English (customization, modernization); British spelling only in the video guidelines
- Spell out acronyms once: "Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)"

### 16.4 CTA Wording (as used)

| Context | Copy |
|---|---|
| Header / page-level primary | **Book a 30-Min Call** (header), **Book a 30-Min Call →** (band) |
| Secondary | **Let's Connect** · **See our work** |
| Service exploration | **Explore AI & Data →** · **Explore Odoo services →** · **Add AI to your Odoo →** · **All services →** |
| Delivery model | **See how we build →** · **See our managed process →** |
| Proof | **View all case studies →** · **Clutch verified reviews ↗** |
| Blog | **Read more →** |
| Form submit | **Send message →** |

**Avoid:** "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more", "Talk to Experts" (retired), "Get Started" (vague).

### 16.5 Eyebrow Vocabulary

`WHAT WE DO` · `HOW WE DELIVER` · `AI & DATA` · `OUR WORK` · `WHY BIZTECH` · `INDUSTRIES` · `PROCESS` · `ENGAGEMENT MODELS` · `EVENTS` · `TECHNOLOGIES` · `FAQ` · `INSIGHTS` · `NEXT STEP`

### 16.6 Trust Language (use verbatim)

- "NDA on request · ISO 27001-certified processes · your data stays yours."
- "We reply within one business day."
- "Full code ownership transferred to you on completion."
- "We sign Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) on request … and handle EU client data in line with GDPR."

### 16.7 Do's and Don'ts

**Do:** open with the reader's risk; give the number; end with one next step; name real clients and real outcomes ("25% more repeat orders", "75% fewer no-shows").
**Don't:** chain buzzwords; hedge ("might", "potentially"); start three sentences in a row with "We"; promise durations or prices as delivery guarantees.

## 18. Implementation Notes

### 18.1 Order of work

1. Drop the `:root` block from Section 17 into `tokens.css`
2. Self-host Inter 400/500/600/700 as `woff2` with `font-display: swap`; preload the 400 and 700 files
3. Base styles: `body { font: 400 16px/1.5 var(--font-body); color: var(--text); background: var(--paper); }`, headings in `--ink` with `text-transform: capitalize` on h1–h4

### 18.3 Responsive expectations

- Mobile-first; the two breakpoints that matter are **640px** and **1080px**
- Fluid headings via the `clamp()` tokens; never set a fixed H1 size
- Grids: `repeat(4, minmax(0, 1fr))` → `repeat(2, …)` ≤ 1080px → `1fr` ≤ 640px
- Header height stays 72 px; only the nav collapses

### 18.4 Performance

- Fonts self-hosted, subset by `unicode-range`, swap
- `content-visibility: auto; contain-intrinsic-size: auto 800px` on the footer
- `.reveal` only animates `opacity` and `transform`
- Print stylesheet hides header, footer, forms, widgets and un-hides reveals

### 18.5 Naming

CSS classes are **short, flat, hyphenated** (`.tv-card`, `.f-hub`, `.svc-sub`), not BEM. Tokens are `--kebab-case`. Files are kebab-case. Keep that convention when extending the site; use BEM only in separate product UIs.

## 19. Do's and Don'ts

### 19.1 Visual

| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|---|---|
| Alternate paper → mist → dark gradient sections | Stack two dark bands, or two mist sections |
| Use `18px` card radius and `11px` button radius everywhere | Mix 6px, 8px and 12px radii from the old system |
| Use long soft teal-tinted shadows | Use hard grey drop shadows |
| Show proof: stat bar, badges, grayscale logos | Use decorative stock imagery to fill space |
| Keep teal glows subtle (≤ 13 % alpha) | Use large saturated teal backgrounds |

### 19.2 Typography

| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|---|---|
| Inter only, weights 400–700 | Reintroduce Poppins, or use weight 800 on the web |
| Use the `clamp()` scale for H1 / H2 | Hard-code 56 px headings |
| Tight tracking on H1 / H2 (`-0.025em` / `-0.02em`) | Track headings positively |
| Uppercase only on 13–14 px eyebrows and pills | Uppercase body copy or buttons |
| Keep H2 ≤ 40ch and lead ≤ 82ch | Let titles run full width |

### 19.3 Colour

| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|---|---|
| `--indigo` for every button, link and interactive stroke | Use `--teal` for text on white |
| `--ink` for headings and dark bands | Use pure black `#000` or the old navy `#0B1F3A` |
| `--muted` for secondary text | Use `#6B7280` or other greys from the old palette |
| Reserve amber `#C9772F` for stat callouts | Use amber, orange or yellow for buttons |
| Test contrast for every text / background pair | Assume teal passes on light surfaces |

### 19.4 Buttons & CTAs

| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|---|---|
| One `.btn-primary` per section, ghost beside it | Two primaries side by side |
| "Book a 30-Min Call" as the page-level CTA | "Talk to Experts", "Get Started", "Submit" |
| `→` arrow after labels that go somewhere | Arrows on "Let's Connect" or form submits without destination |
| Full-width primary inside forms | Secondary / ghost submit buttons |

### 19.5 Content

| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|---|---|
| Lead with the risk, then the outcome | Lead with company history |
| Short two-beat headlines with full stops | Headlines with exclamation marks or em-dash flourishes |
| Real names, real numbers (TVS Motor, 25%, 75%) | "A leading retailer", "significant gains" |
| Use the footer descriptor as the brand line | Use "Tech that works, so you don't have to." |
| American spelling on the web | Mixed spelling across pages |

Founded in 2006 in Ahmedabad by Maulik Shah, Biztech is a 200-person, ISO 27001 / 9001-certified software engineering and products company with a US sales office in Wilmington, Delaware. ### What the Brand Stands For

**AI-forward, outcome-owned.** The redesign puts AI first and promises production, not pilots, with one partner accountable at every layer.

### How the Visual System Works

- **Ink** `#10262B` is the authority: headings, the stat bar, dark bands, the footer
- **Action teal** `#0E7C88` is the only interactive colour: buttons, links, kickers, icon strokes, focus
- **Bright teal** `#35B0BF` is the signature: the logo mark, stat units, eyebrows on dark, hover accents
- **Paper and mist** give the page air; hairline `#E4E8F0` borders do the structural work
- **Inter**, four weights, tight tracking on titles, does everything from the H1 to the copyright line
